always / usually / often
1. Mid-position (Fe'l bilan birga)
Always, usually, often, never, rarely, sometimes kabi ravishlar gapning
o'rtasida, ya'ni asosiy fe'ldan oldin keladi.
Subject + Adverb + Verb
• I always drink coffee in the morning.
• Helen usually goes to work by car.
• They often watch television in the evening.
• I never eat meat.
• Do you often go to the cinema?
2. With 'BE' (am / is / are)
Agar gapda BE fe'li (am/is/are) bo'lsa, ravish undan keyin keladi.
am / is / are + Adverb
• I am always tired.
• They are usually at home on Sundays.
• It is often cold in winter.
• You are never on time!
3. With Auxiliary Verbs (Yordamchi fe'llar)
Agar gapda yordamchi fe'l (have, can, will) bo'lsa, ravish ular orasida keladi.
• I have always lived in this city.
• You can never remember my name!
• We will usually be here at 9:00.

still, yet, already
1. STILL (Hali ham)
Still biror ish-harakatning o'zgarishsiz davom etayotganini bildiradi. Odatda
gapning o'rtasida (fe'ldan oldin) keladi.
• It is still raining. (Hali ham yomg'ir yog'yapti)
• Do you still live in London? (Hali ham Londonda yashaysizmi?)
2. YET (Hali / Haliyam)
Yet kutilgan ishning hali sodir bo'lmaganini bildiradi. Inkor va so'roq gaplarda,
gapning oxirida keladi.
• Has the bus arrived yet? (Avtobus keldimi hali?)
• I haven't finished my homework yet. (Uy vazifamni hali tugatmadim)
3. ALREADY (Allaqachon)
Already biror ish kutilganidan vaqtliroq sodir bo'lganini bildiradi. Gapning
o'rtasida keladi.
• "What time is Joe coming?" "He is already here."
• I've already paid the bill. (Hisobni allaqachon to'ladim)

from ... to, until, since, for
1. FROM ... TO ( ... dan ... gacha)
Biz biror ishning qachon boshlanib qachon tugaganini aytish uchun ishlatamiz.
• We lived in Japan from 2010 to 2015.
• I work from Monday to Friday.
2. UNTIL ( ... gacha)
Ishning ma'lum bir vaqt nuqtasigacha davom etishini bildiradi.
• Let's wait until it stops raining.
• I stayed in bed until half past nine.
3. SINCE ( ... dan beri)
Ishning boshlanish nuqtasini ko'rsatadi (asosan Present Perfect bilan).
• We have lived in this house since 2018.
• It has been raining since I got up.
4. FOR ( ... davomida)
Vaqtning umumiy davomiyligini (qancha vaqt?) ko'rsatadi.
• We have lived here for three days.
• She is going away for a week.

before, after, during, while
1. BEFORE and AFTER (... oldin / ... keyin)
Ushbu predloglardan keyin ot (noun) yoki -ing shaklidagi fe'l kelishi mumkin.
• Everybody was nervous before the exam.
• I'll call you after work.
• Wash your hands before eating.
2. DURING (... davomida)
During predlogidan keyin har doim ot (noun) keladi. U biror narsa qachon sodir
bo'lganini bildiradi.
• I fell asleep during the film.
• We met a lot of people during our holiday.
3. WHILE (... davomida / -ayotgan vaqtda)
While bog'lovchisidan keyin har doim Ega + Fe'l (sentence) keladi.
• I fell asleep while I was watching the film.
• We met a lot of people while we were on holiday.
