UNIT 94
still, yet, already
Hali ham, hali, allaqachon

STILL (Hali ham)
Still biror ish-harakatning o'zgarishsiz davom etayotganini bildiradi. Odatda gapning o'rtasida (fe'ldan oldin) keladi.
Examples:
• It is still raining. (Hali ham yomg'ir yog'yapti)
• Do you still live in London? (Hali ham Londonda yashaysizmi?)

YET (Hali / Haliyam)
Yet kutilgan ishning hali sodir bo'lmaganini bildiradi. Inkor va so'roq gaplarda, gapning oxirida keladi.
Examples:
• Has the bus arrived yet? (Avtobus keldimi hali?)
• I haven't finished my homework yet. (Uy vazifamni hali tugatmadim)

ALREADY (Allaqachon)
Already biror ish kutilganidan vaqtliroq sodir bo'lganini bildiradi. Gapning o'rtasida keladi.
Examples:
• "What time is Joe coming?" "He is already here."
• I've already paid the bill. (Hisobni allaqachon to'ladim)

Exercises (Homework)
94.1 Put in STILL, YET or ALREADY.
1. I'm hungry. Is dinner ready yet?
2. It's 11 o'clock and Joe is still in bed.
3. I've already had three cups of coffee this morning.
4. Do you still work in the same place?
5. Has it stopped raining yet?

94.2 Write sentences with YET.
1. (she / not / arrive) → She hasn't arrived yet.
2. (they / not / finish / their dinner) → They haven't finished their dinner yet.
3. (it / not / stop / raining) → It hasn't stopped raining yet.

94.3 Compare STILL and YET.
• He still hasn't found a job. (Hali ham topmadi - holat davom etyapti)
• He hasn't found a job yet. (Hali topmadi - yaqin orada topishini kutadi)
