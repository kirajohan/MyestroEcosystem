# CEFR darajalari (Common European Framework of Reference)

CEFR — xalqaro standart bo'lib, til bilish darajasini 6 bosqichga bo'ladi.

## A1 — Beginner (Boshlang'ich)
**Siz nima qila olasiz:**
- Oddiy salomlasha olasiz
- O'zingizni tanishtira olasiz
- Oddiy savollarga javob bera olasiz (qayerda yashaysiz, nima ish qilasiz)
- Sekin va aniq gapirilgan so'zlarni tushunasiz

**Misol:**
"Hello, my name is John. I am from Uzbekistan. I am a student."

## A2 — Elementary (Boshlang'ich+)
**Siz nima qila olasiz:**
- Kundalik mavzularda suhbatlasha olasiz
- Oddiy matnlarni o'qiy olasiz
- Qisqa xabarlar yoza olasiz
- Do'kon, restoranda muloqot qila olasiz

**Misol:**
"Yesterday I went to the shop. I bought some bread and milk. The weather was nice."

## B1 — Intermediate (O'rta)
**Siz nima qila olasiz:**
- Ko'pchilik kundalik vaziyatlarda muloqot qila olasiz
- Sayohat paytida muammolarni hal qila olasiz
- Shaxsiy tajribangiz haqida gapira olasiz
- Oddiy matnlarning asosiy mazmunini tushunasiz

**Misol:**
"If I had more time, I would travel to Europe. I have always wanted to visit Paris and London."

## B2 — Upper Intermediate (O'rta+)
**Siz nima qila olasiz:**
- Murakkab mavzularda gaplasha olasiz
- Professional muhitda muloqot qila olasiz
- Tabiiy tezlikda gaplashuvchilarni tushunasiz
- Batafsil matnlar yoza olasiz

**Misol:**
"Despite the challenges, I believe that technology has significantly improved our daily lives. However, we must consider its environmental impact."

## C1 — Advanced (Yuqori)
**Siz nima qila olasiz:**
- Erkin va oqilona ifodalanish
- Akademik va professional matnlarni tushunish
- Nozik ma'nolarni anglash
- Spontan suhbatlarda ishtirok etish

**Misol:**
"Notwithstanding the prevailing economic conditions, the company has managed to sustain its growth trajectory through strategic diversification."

## C2 — Proficiency (Mukammal)
**Siz nima qila olasiz:**
- Har qanday mavzuda erkin gaplashish
- Ona tiliga yaqin daraja
- Murakkab adabiy asarlarni tushunish
- Nozik uslubiy farqlarni anglash

## Myestro'da darajangizni qanday bilasiz?
1. Profil bo'limida darajangizni tanlang
2. Imtihon topshiring — AI sizning haqiqiy darajangizni aniqlaydi
3. Progress bo'limida o'sishingizni kuzating

## Qaysi daraja sizga kerak?
- **Sayohat uchun:** A2
- **Ish uchun:** B1-B2
- **Universitet uchun:** B2-C1
- **IELTS 7+ uchun:** C1
