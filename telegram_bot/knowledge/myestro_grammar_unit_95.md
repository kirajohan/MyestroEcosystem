UNIT 95
Give me that book!
Ikki to'ldiruvchi bilan fe'llar

Verb + Person + Thing
Ba'zi fe'llardan (give, lend, send, show, buy) keyin ikkita to'ldiruvchi kelishi mumkin. Birinchi shaxs, keyin narsa keladi.
Examples:
• Give me (person) that book (thing).
• I lent Joe some money.
• Did you send her a postcard?

Verb + Thing + to/for + Person
Agar narsa birinchi kelsa, shaxsdan oldin "to" yoki "for" predlogi ishlatilishi shart.
Examples:
• Give it (thing) to me (person).
• I lent some money to Joe.
• He bought some flowers for his mother.

Pronouns (it / them)
Agar narsa olmosh (it/them) bo'lsa, u odatda birinchi keladi va to/for ishlatiladi.
Examples:
• I gave it to Joe. (NOT: I gave Joe it)
• Here are the keys. Give them to your father.

Exercises (Homework)
95.1 Put the words in the right order.
1. (me / give / that book) → Give me that book.
2. (to / give / me / it) → Give it to me.
3. (him / lent / I / money / some) → I lent him some money.
4. (buy / for / flowers / her / some) → Buy some flowers for her.
5. (show / your photos / me) → Show me your photos.

95.2 Rewrite these sentences. Change the order.
1. I gave the keys to Sarah. → I gave Sarah the keys.
2. He sent a message to me. → He sent me a message.
3. I bought a present for Joe. → I bought Joe a present.
4. Show your new car to us. → Show us your new car.

95.3 Complete the sentences with "it" or "them".
1. I lent my book to him. I lent it to him.
2. I showed my photos to her. I showed them to her.
3. Give the money to me. Give it to me.
