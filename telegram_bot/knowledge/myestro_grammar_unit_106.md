UNIT 106
Prepositions after Nouns
Ot dan keyin keladigan predloglar

Ko'pgina otlar o'zidan keyin ma'lum bir predlogni talab qiladi. Bu odatda mos keladigan sifat yoki fe'ldagi predlog bilan bir xil bo'ladi.

advantage of (afzalligi)
• The advantage of living in the city is the convenience.

cause of (sababi)
• The cause of the fire is still unknown.

damage to (zarar)
• The storm caused a lot of damage to the building.

increase in (o'sish)
• There has been a big increase in prices.

interest in (qiziqish)
• She has no interest in politics.

reason for (sabab)
• What is the reason for your decision?

relationship between (o'rtasidagi munosabat)
• The relationship between the two countries is improving.

solution to (yechim)
• We need to find a solution to this problem.

Exercises (Homework)
106.1 Complete the sentences with OF, TO, IN or FOR.
1. What is the reason for your decision?
2. There has been a big increase in crime.
3. The storm caused a lot of damage to the roof.
4. She has a good relationship with her colleagues.
5. We need to find a solution to the problem.
6. The cause of the accident was bad weather.

106.2 Rewrite the sentences using the noun form.
1. I'm interested in art. → I have an interest in art.
2. Prices have increased. → There has been an increase in prices.
3. This solves the problem. → This is a solution to the problem.
