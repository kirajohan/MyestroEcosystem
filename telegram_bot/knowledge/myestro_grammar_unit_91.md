UNIT 91
too
Keragidan ortiq / Juda ham

too + Adjective/Adverb
Too sifat yoki ravishdan oldin kelib, biror narsaning me'yordan ko'pligini va bu odatda muammo ekanligini bildiradi.
Formula: too + adjective / adverb

Examples:
• Can you turn the radio down? It's too loud.
• I can't work. I'm too tired.
• I think you work too hard.
• This jacket is too small for me.

too much / too many
Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan miqdorni ifodalash:
• too much + uncountable: I put too much sugar in my tea.
• too many + countable: There are too many people on the bus.

too ... to / too ... for
• too ... for somebody: These shoes are too big for me.
• too ... to do something: I'm too tired to go out tonight.
• It's too cold to sit outside.

Compare: very vs too
• It is very hot, but I can drink it.
• It is too hot. I can't drink it. (Too = muammo bor)

Exercises (Homework)
91.1 Put in TOO or ENOUGH.
1. You can't reach the shelf. You aren't tall enough.
2. You can't reach the shelf. You are too short.
3. I can't go out. I'm too busy.
4. I don't have enough money to buy a car.
5. This jacket is too small for me.

91.2 Put in TOO MUCH or TOO MANY.
1. I don't like this town. There is too much traffic.
2. There were too many people at the party.
3. You put too much salt in the soup.
4. He watches TV too much.
5. I've got too much work to do.

91.3 Complete with TOO ... TO.
(tired / go out) I'm too tired to go out.
(busy / talk) I'm too busy to talk to you now.
(cold / sit outside) It's too cold to sit outside.
(late / go to the bank) It's too late to go to the bank.
