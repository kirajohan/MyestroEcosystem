UNIT
100
in, at, on
Places (2) - Joy predloglari
1. IN (Holat va yopiq makonlar)
In predlogi asosan biror narsaning ichida yoki muayyan holatda (bed, hospital)
ekanligini bildiradi.
• in bed: 'Where's Kate?' 'She's in bed.'
• in hospital / in prison: He's in hospital.
• in a street: I live in a small street near the station.
• in the sky / in the world: The largest city in the world.
• in a newspaper / in a book: I read it in the newspaper.
2. AT (Ijtimoiy joylar va binolar)
At predlogi ijtimoiy muassasalar, tadbirlar yoki aniq xizmat ko'rsatish nuqtalari
uchun ishlatiladi.
• at home / at work / at school: She's at work.
• at university / at college: Helen is studying at university.
• at the station / at the airport: Meet me at the station.
• at a party / at a concert: There weren't many people at the party.
3. ON (Transport va qavatlar)
• on a bus / on a train / on a plane: He is on a bus. •
on the first floor: My office is on the first floor.
• on the way: I met him on the way home.
