UNIT
92
always / usually / often
1. Mid-position (Fe'l bilan birga)
Always, usually, often, never, rarely, sometimes kabi ravishlar gapning
o'rtasida, ya'ni asosiy fe'ldan oldin keladi.
Subject + Adverb + Verb
• I always drink coffee in the morning.
• Helen usually goes to work by car.
• They often watch television in the evening.
• I never eat meat.
• Do you often go to the cinema?
2. With 'BE' (am / is / are)
Agar gapda BE fe'li (am/is/are) bo'lsa, ravish undan keyin keladi.
am / is / are + Adverb
• I am always tired.
• They are usually at home on Sundays.
• It is often cold in winter.
• You are never on time!
3. With Auxiliary Verbs (Yordamchi fe'llar)
Agar gapda yordamchi fe'l (have, can, will) bo'lsa, ravish ular orasida keladi.
• I have always lived in this city.
• You can never remember my name!
• We will usually be here at 9:00.
