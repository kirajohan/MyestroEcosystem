UNIT
97
from ... to, until, since, for
1. FROM ... TO ( ... dan ... gacha)
Biz biror ishning qachon boshlanib qachon tugaganini aytish uchun ishlatamiz.
• We lived in Japan from 2010 to 2015.
• I work from Monday to Friday.
2. UNTIL ( ... gacha)
Ishning ma'lum bir vaqt nuqtasigacha davom etishini bildiradi.
• Let's wait until it stops raining.
• I stayed in bed until half past nine.
3. SINCE ( ... dan beri)
Ishning boshlanish nuqtasini ko'rsatadi (asosan Present Perfect bilan).
• We have lived in this house since 2018.
• It has been raining since I got up.
4. FOR ( ... davomida)
Vaqtning umumiy davomiyligini (qancha vaqt?) ko'rsatadi.
• We have lived here for three days.
• She is going away for a week.
