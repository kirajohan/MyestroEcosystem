102
under, behind, opposite
1. UNDER, BEHIND & IN FRONT OF
Narsalarning bir-biriga nisbatan joylashishini ko'rsatuvchi asosiy predloglar:
| UNDER |     | BEHIND |     | IN FRONT OF |
| ----- | --- | ------ | --- | ----------- |
[Image of a ball under a [Image of a dog behind a [Image of a car in front of
| table] |     | fence] |     | a garage] |
| ------ | --- | ------ | --- | --------- |
• The cat is under the table.
• Who is that man standing behind the tree?
• There is a beautiful garden in front of our house.
2. NEXT TO, BESIDE, BETWEEN
Yonma-yon yoki o'rtada bo'lish holatlari:
| NEXT TO | BETWEEN |     |     | OPPOSITE |
| ------- | ------- | --- | --- | -------- |
[Image of a house next to [Image of a book between [Image of two chairs
| a tree] | two lamps] |     | opposite each other] |     |
| ------- | ---------- | --- | -------------------- | --- |
• Come and sit next to (beside) me.
• Our house is between the bank and the shop.
• The cinema is opposite the park (yo'lning narigi tomonida).
