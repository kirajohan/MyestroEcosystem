1. Verb + Preposition (Essential Pairs)
Ingliz tilida ko'plab fe'llar faqat o'ziga xos predloglar bilan birga ishlatiladi. Bu
birikmalarni o'zgarmas holatda yodlash lozim:

• I like listening to music.
• Don't look at me like that!
• I'm waiting for the rain to stop.
• Did you talk to Joe yesterday?
• She is thinking about her holiday.
2. Dependent Prepositions (Common Verbs)
• Ask for (so'ramoq): He asked for some money.
• Belong to (...ga tegishli bo'lmoq): Does this book belong to you?
• Happen to (sodir bo'lmoq): What happened to your car?
• Speak to / Talk to (...bilan gaplashmoq): Can I speak to Jane, please?
Eslatma:"Listen", "Look", "Wait" kabi fe'llardan keyin to'ldiruvchi kelsa, predlog
shart!
