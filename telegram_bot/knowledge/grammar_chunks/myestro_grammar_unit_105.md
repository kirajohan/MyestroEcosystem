• Are you afraid of dogs?
• She is very good at playing the piano.
• Italy is famous for its art and food.
• I'm interested in learning English.
• The room was full of people.
2. Preposition + -ing (The Gerund Rule)
Predlogdan (at, of, in, with, etc.) keyin har doim fe'lning -ing shakli keladi:
• I'm not very good at telling jokes.
• Are you interested in working in London?
• I'm fed up with waiting for him.
• Thank you for helping me.
