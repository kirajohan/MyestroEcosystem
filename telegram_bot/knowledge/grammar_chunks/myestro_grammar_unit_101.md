UNIT
101
to, in, at
Places(3)-Yo'nalishva Joylashuv
TO (Direction) IN / AT (Position)
Harakat va yo'nalishni (qayerga?) Harakatsiz holatni (qayerda?)
bildiradi. bildiradi.
go / come / return / walk to... be / stay / do something in/
at...
• We're going to London.
• I want to go to Italy. • Piccadilly Circus is in
• We walked to the centre. London.
• What time do you go to bed? • My brother lives in Italy.
• I went to a party. • I like reading in bed.
• The bus is at the airport.
• I met her at a party.

Special Cases (Maxsus holatlar)
• HOME: "Home" so'zi bilan to ishlatilmaydi: go home / come home. Lekin
joylashuvda: at home.
• ARRIVE:Arrive in (davlat/shahar) / Arrive at (bino/joy).
• GET: Har doim to ishlatiladi: get to work / get to the hotel.
