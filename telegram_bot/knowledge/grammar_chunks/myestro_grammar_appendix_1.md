Appendix 1: Exercises
A1.1 Gaplarni Passive (majhul nisbat) shakliga o'tkazing.
1. Somebody cleans the room every day. → The room is cleaned every day.
2. They are building a new airport. →
3. Somebody stole my keys yesterday. →
4. They have washed the shirts. →
5. Somebody will repair the car. →
6. Ann said that somebody had stolen her car. →
7. You should wash this sweater by hand. →
A1.2 Active yoki Passive: Fe'llarni to'g'ri shaklda yozing.
1. This house was built (build) 100 years ago.
2. Football (play) in most countries.
3. Why (the letter / not / send) yesterday?
4. A new supermarket (build) near my house at the moment.
5. When I arrived, the room (clean).
6. I (invite) to a party last week but I didn't go.
7. "Is this shirt clean?" "Yes, it (wash)."
