UNIT
114
the people we met...
1. Omitting "who / that / which"
Aniqlovchi ergashg aplarda who / that / which so'zlarini tushirib qoldirish mumkin,
agar ular gapda to'ldiruvchi (object) vazifasida kelsa:
BOG'LOVCHI BILAN BOG'LOVCHISIZ (Ko'p ishlatiladi)
The people who we
The peoplew e met...
The hotel which you stayed at...
The hotel you stayed at...
The film that I watched...
The film I watched...
2. Prepositions in Relative Clauses
Agarf e'ldankeyinpredlog(at,to,for,with)kelsa,bog'lovchi tushib qolganda predlog
o'z joyida qoladi:
• Are these the books (that) you were looking for?
• The girl (who) I was talking to is my sister.
• The hotel (which) we stayed at was very expensive.
⚠
Muhim Qoida: Agar bog'lovchi gapda ega (subject) vazifasida kelsa (fe'ldan
oldin tursa), uni tushirib qoldirib bo'lmaydi!
• The woman who lives next door is a doctor. (Bu yerda "who"ni tushirish mumkin
emas).
