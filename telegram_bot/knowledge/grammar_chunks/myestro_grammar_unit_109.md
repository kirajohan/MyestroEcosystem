UNIT
109
and, but, or, so, because
1. AND, BUT and OR
Ushbub og'lovchilargapqismlarinibirlashtirish uchun xizmat qiladi:
AND BUT
Ziddiyat/Farq
Fikrni qo'shish
"Nice but small"
"Tea and coffee"
• AND: We stayed at home and watched TV.
• BUT: I bought a newspaper, but I didn't read it.
• OR: Do you want to go out, or are you too tired?
2. SO and BECAUSE
Sabab va natijani ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi:
SO BECAUSE
Natija (Result) Sabab (Reason)
Hot → so → Open window Open window → because → Hot
• SO: It was very hot, so I opened the window.
• BECAUSE: I opened the window because it was very hot.
