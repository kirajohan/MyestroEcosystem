⚠
MUHIM QOIDA: Olmoshlar bilan (it/them)
Agar to'ldiruvchi olmosh (it/them) bo'lsa, u har doim fe'l va ravish o'rtasida keladi:
Verb + IT / THEM + Particle
• Put it on. (NOT put on it)
• Take them off. (NOT take off them)
