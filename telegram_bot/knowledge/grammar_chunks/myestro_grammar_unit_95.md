UNIT
95
Give me that book!
1. Verb + Person + Thing
Ba'zi fe'llardan (give, lend, send, show, buy) keyin ikkita to'ldiruvchi kelishi
mumkin. Birinchi shaxs, keyin narsa keladi.
• Give me (person) that book (thing).
• I lent Joe some money.
• Did you send her a postcard?
2. Verb + Thing + to/for + Person
Agar narsa birinchi kelsa, shaxsdan oldin to yoki for predlogi ishlatilishi shart.
• Give it (thing) to me (person).
• I lent some moneyto Joe.
• He bought some flowersfor his mother.
3. Pronouns (it / them)
Agar narsa olmosh (it/them) bo'lsa, u odatda birinchi keladi va to/for ishlatiladi.
• I gave itto Joe. (NOT I gave Joe it)
• Here are the keys. Give themto your father.
