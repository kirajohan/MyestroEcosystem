UNIT
110
When ...
1. Structure & Punctuation (Vergul qoidasi)
"When" ishtiroketgangaplarikkiqismdaniboratbo'ladi.Ularningtartibi o'zgarishi
mumkin:
WHEN BILAN BOSHLANSA (Vergul bor) ASOSIY GAP BILAN BOSHLANSA (Vergul
yo'q)
When I went out,itwasraining.
It wasrainingwhen I wentout.
Agarg apboshida"When"kelsa,o'rtaga vergul
qo'yiladi. Agar" When"o'rtadakelsa,vergulshart emas.
Bu qoida before, while va after bog'lovchilari uchun ham amal qiladi: •
Before you cross the road, always look both ways.
• Always look both ways before you cross the road.
2. Future: When I do ... (Kelasi zamon uchun)
Kelasiz amonhaqidagapirganda"when"qismidawillishlatilmaydi.Buning o'rniga
Present Simple ishlatiladi:
Jill next week New Yorkda bo'ladi. Barbara esa Meksikada.
• Barbara will be in Mexico when Jill is in New York.
(❌ NOT when Jill will be)
• I'll phone you when I get home. (❌ NOT when I will get)

Exercises (Homework)
110.1 Gaplarni vergul qoidasiga rioya qilib qaytayo zing.
1. (when / Jill / pass her exam / she / be very happy) →
When Jill passes her exam, she will be very happy.
2. (Jill / be very happy / when / she / pass her exam) →
Jill will be very happy when she passes her exam.
3. (when / you / be tired / don't drive) →
4. (don't drive / when / you / be tired) →
5. (before / you / go out / close the window) →
110.2 Fe'llarni to'g'ri shaklda qo'ying (Will yoki Present
Simple).
1. I'll stay here until you
come (come) back.
2. I'll phone you when I
(get) home.
3. When I

4. Don't worry. I
(be) careful when I cross the road.
5. I'll be very happy when I
(pass) my driving test.
6. We
