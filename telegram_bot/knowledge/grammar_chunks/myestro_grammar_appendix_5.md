Appendix 5
MyEstro
Phrasal Verbs(lookout/takeoffetc.)
Eng muhim frazeologik fe'llar ro'yxati (Unit 107 bilan bog'liq).
out look out / watch out= be careful
Look out! There's a car coming!
[Illustration: Car & Pedestrian]
comeo n
on = hurry / be quick
Come on! Everybody is waiting for you.
[Illustration: Telephone Wait]
hold on
= wait
Can you hold on a minute?
carry on / go on
= continue
Don't stop working. Carry on.
take off
off = leave the ground (planes)
The plane took off 20 minutes late.
[Illustration: Plane Taking Off]
wake up/speakup
up = stop sleeping / louder
I often wake up in the middle of the night.
[Illustration: Growing Up]
wash up / grow up
= wash plates / become adult
What does your son want to do when he grows up?
give up
= stop trying
I know it's difficult but don't give up.
slow down / break down
down = go slowly / stop working
Sue was late because her car broke down.
[Illustration: Car Breaking
Down]
