2. WHICH / THAT (for things and animals)
Narsalary okihayvonlar haqidagapirgandawhich yokithat ishlatamiz:
WHICH / THAT
• An airplane is a machine that
flies.
[Image of a machine or object]

Logic Tip: So'zlashuv tilida odamlar uchun ham that ishlatish mumkin (the

113.2  Ikki  gapni  WHO,  WHICH  yoki  THAT  yordamida  bitta  gapga
aylantiring.
1. A man phoned. He didn't give his name.
→ The man who phoned didn't give his name.
2. A car was stolen. It was found near the station.
→ The car that was stolen was found near the station.
3. A woman lives next door. She is a doctor.
→
4. Some pictures are on the wall. They are very beautiful.
→
5. A girl won the race. She is my cousin.
→
6. I bought a machine. It doesn't work.
