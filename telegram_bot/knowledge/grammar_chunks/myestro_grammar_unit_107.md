UNIT
107
go in, fall off, run away
Phrasal verb - bu fe'l (go/look/get) va ravish (in/out/up/down)b irikmasi.
IN / OUT ON / OFF
• Ann opened the door and got in. • The bus arrived and I got on.
• I went to the window and looked out. • Be careful! Don't fall off.
UP / DOWN AWAY / BACK
• He stood up and left the room. • The thief ran away (or off).
• Would you like to sit down? • Go away and don't come back!
OVER ROUND (AROUND)
• We climbed over the wall. • I looked round to see who it was.
• Turn over to the next page. • We turned round and went back.
