UNIT
93
Word Order (1)
1. Verb + Object (Fe'l va To'ldiruvchi)
Ingliz tilida fe'l va uning to'ldiruvchisi (object) har doim yonma-yon turadi. Biz
ularning orasiga hech qachon boshqa so'z (masalan: very well, every day)
qo'sha olmaymiz.
Subject + Verb + Object
Examples:
• He speaksEnglish very well. (NOT speaks very well English)
• I lost my keys yesterday. (NOT lost yesterday my keys)
• Do you watch television often? (NOT watch often television)
• We ate our dinner quickly.
2. Place and Time (Joy va Vaqt)
Odatda gapda joy (qayerda?) vaqtdan (qachon?) oldin keladi.
Verb + Place + Time
• We went to the cinema yesterday. (NOT yesterday to the cinema)
• They have lived in London for ten years.
• Will you be at home tomorrow?
• I saw her at the party last night.
3. Always Together (Ajralmas birikmalar)
Ushbu holatlarda fe'l va to'ldiruvchi o'rtasiga hech narsa qo'ymang:
• She bought a new car last week.
• I take the bus to work every morning.
