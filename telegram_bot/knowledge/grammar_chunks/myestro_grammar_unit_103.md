103.2 Choose the right preposition.
1. Suddenly a dog ran out of / through the house. →
out of
2. They climbed up / over the wall into the garden.
3. I walked round / through the park to see the flowers.
4. The plane is flying over / down France right now.
💡
Pro Tip: Through vs Round
Through - birorn arsaningichidan o'tish (tunnel, oyna, o'rmon).
Round - biror narsaning atrofidan aylanib o'tish (bino, shahar, dunyo).
