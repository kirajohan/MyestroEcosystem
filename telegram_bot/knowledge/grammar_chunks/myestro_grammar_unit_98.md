UNIT
98
before, after, during, while
1. BEFORE and AFTER (... oldin / ... keyin)
Ushbu predloglardan keyin ot (noun) yoki -ing shaklidagi fe'l kelishi mumkin.
• Everybody was nervous before the exam.
• I'll call you after work.
• Wash your hands before eating.
2. DURING (... davomida)
During predlogidan keyin har doim ot (noun) keladi. U biror narsa qachon sodir
bo'lganini bildiradi.
• I fell asleep during the film.
• We met a lot of people during our holiday.
3. WHILE (... davomida / -ayotgan vaqtda)
While bog'lovchisidan keyin har doim Ega + Fe'l (sentence) keladi.
• I fell asleep while I was watching the film.
• We met a lot of people while we were on holiday.
