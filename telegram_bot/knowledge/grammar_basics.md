# Ingliz tili grammatikasi asoslari

## Zamonlar (Tenses)

### Present Simple — Oddiy hozirgi zamon
**Qachon ishlatiladi:** doimiy harakatlar, faktlar, odatlar
**Tuzilishi:** Subject + V1 (s/es)

**Misol:**
- I work every day. (Men har kuni ishlayman.)
- She speaks English. (U inglizcha gapiradi.)
- They don't like coffee. (Ular kofe yoqtirmaydi.)

**Ko'rsatgich so'zlar:** always, usually, often, sometimes, every day

### Past Simple — Oddiy o'tgan zamon
**Qachon ishlatiladi:** o'tmishda tugagan harakatlar
**Tuzilishi:** Subject + V2

**Misol:**
- I visited London last year. (Men o'tgan yili Londonga bordim.)
- She didn't come yesterday. (U kecha kelmadi.)
- Did you see the movie? (Siz filmni ko'rdingizmi?)

**Ko'rsatgich so'zlar:** yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020

### Present Continuous — Hozirgi davomli zamon
**Qachon ishlatiladi:** hozir sodir bo'layotgan harakatlar
**Tuzilishi:** Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

**Misol:**
- I am studying English now. (Men hozir inglizcha o'rganayapman.)
- They are playing football. (Ular futbol o'ynashyapti.)

**Ko'rsatgich so'zlar:** now, at the moment, currently

### Future Simple — Oddiy kelasi zamon
**Qachon ishlatiladi:** kelajakdagi harakatlar, qarorlar
**Tuzilishi:** Subject + will + V1

**Misol:**
- I will help you. (Men sizga yordam beraman.)
- She will come tomorrow. (U ertaga keladi.)

**Ko'rsatgich so'zlar:** tomorrow, next week, soon

## Artikllar (Articles)

### A/An — noaniq artikl
- Birinchi marta tilga olingan narsa
- Kasb: I am a teacher.
- A (undosh oldidan), An (unli oldidan): a book, an apple

### The — aniq artikl
- Aniq ma'lum narsa: The book on the table
- Yagona narsalar: The sun, The moon
- Superlativ: The best student

## Olmoshlar (Pronouns)
- I, you, he, she, it, we, they
- My, your, his, her, its, our, their
- Me, you, him, her, it, us, them

## Ko'plik (Plural)
- Oddiy: + s (book → books)
- -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o bilan tugasa: + es (bus → buses)
- -y bilan tugasa (undosh + y): -ies (baby → babies)
- Istisnolar: man → men, child → children, foot → feet

## Yordamchi fe'llar (Modal Verbs)
- **can** — qila olish: I can swim.
- **must** — majburiyat: You must study.
- **should** — maslahat: You should rest.
- **would** — istak/shart: I would like tea.
