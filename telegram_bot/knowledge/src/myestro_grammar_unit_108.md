UNIT 108
Verb + Preposition (2)
Fe'l va undan keyin keladigan predloglar (Davomi)

believe in (ishonmoq - mavjudligiga yoki to'g'riligiga)
• Do you believe in ghosts?
• I believe in you. (Senga ishonaman)

care about (qayg'urmoq / ahamiyat bermoq)
• He doesn't care about money.

complain about (shikoyat qilmoq)
• They complained about the noise.

dream about (orzu qilmoq / tush ko'rmoq)
• I dreamt about you last night.

insist on (qat'iy talab qilmoq)
• She insisted on paying for the meal.

laugh at (kulmoq - kimnidir masxara qilib)
• Don't laugh at him. It's not funny.

rely on (tayanmoq / ishonmoq)
• You can rely on me. I won't tell anyone.

succeed in (muvaffaqiyat qozonmoq)
• He succeeded in passing the exam.

think about / of (o'ylamoq / fikrlamoq)
• What are you thinking about?

Exercises (Homework)
108.1 Complete the sentences with the correct preposition.
1. I don't care about what other people think.
2. They complained about the bad service.
3. You can rely on me to help you.
4. He succeeded in finding a good job.
5. Please don't laugh at my mistakes.
6. She insisted on driving me home.

108.2 Write sentences using the words in brackets.
1. (dream / last night) → I dreamt about my childhood last night.
2. (believe / hard work) → I believe in hard work.
3. (think / holiday) → I am thinking about my next holiday.

💡 Muhim farq:
Think about - biror narsa haqida o'ylash jarayoni.
Think of - biror narsa haqida fikr bildirish yoki eslash (What do you think of this idea?).
