UNIT 99
in, at, on
Places (1) - Joy predloglari

IN (Ichida / Ichkarida)
In predlogi chegaralangan maydon, bino, xona yoki geografik hududlar uchun ishlatiladi.
Examples:
• "Where is David?" "In the kitchen / In the garden."
• Angela works in a bank / in a factory.
• I had a swim in the river / in the sea.
• Milan is in the north of Italy.

AT (Oldida / Nuqtada)
At predlogi aniq bir nuqta, buyum yonida yoki uchrashuv joyida bo'lishni bildiradi.
Examples:
• at the bus stop
• at the door
• at her desk

ON (Ustida / Yuzasida)
On predlogi biror narsaning yuzasida joylashgan holatni bildiradi.
Examples:
• There are some beautiful pictures on the wall.
• Don't sit on the floor. It is dirty.
• There is a label on the bottle.

Exercises (Homework)
99.1 Put in IN, AT or ON.
1. Write your name at the top of the page.
2. I have a swim in the river every morning.
3. There is somebody at the bus stop.
4. Who is that man in that photo?
5. There are some beautiful pictures on the wall.
6. Milan is in the north of Italy.
7. The car is waiting at the traffic lights.

99.2 Complete the sentences with IN, AT or ON + words below.
[the garden / her desk / the ceiling / the end of the street]
1. "Where is David?" "He is in the garden."
2. "Where is Jane?" "She is at her desk."
3. There is a light on the ceiling.
4. Our house is at the end of the street.
