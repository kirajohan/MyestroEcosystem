UNIT 104
on, at, by, with, about
Turli vaziyatlar uchun predloglar

ON (Transport va hordiq)
• ON holiday: We are going on holiday next week.
• ON television: Did you see the news on television?
• ON the phone: I spoke to her on the phone.

AT (Yosh va narx)
Aniq miqdor yoki yoshni ko'rsatish uchun:
• She left school at 16.
• Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
• The car was travelling at 120 km/h.

BY (Transport va mualliflik)
• BY car / bus: I'm going to London by train.
• BY credit card: Can you pay by credit card in this shop?
• BY Shakespeare: This book was written by Shakespeare.

WITH and ABOUT
• WITH (asbob yoki birga): Did you stay with friends? / Cut it with a knife.
• ABOUT (mavzu haqida): Tell me about your trip. / A book about animals.

Exercises (Homework)
104.1 Gaplarni ON, AT, BY, WITH yoki ABOUT bilan to'ldiring.
1. I'm going to London by train.
2. I cut my finger with a knife.
3. I don't know much about politics.
4. We are going on holiday to Spain.
5. Can you pay by credit card in this shop?
6. Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
7. Did you see the film on television?
8. Mozart started writing music at the age of five.
9. We met some people at a party.
10. He is on the phone at the moment.

💡 Muhim: BY vs WITH
BY - transport yoki to'lov vositasi (by bus, by card).
WITH - asbob yoki birga bo'lish (with a hammer, with friends).
