UNIT 102
under, behind, opposite
Narsalarning bir-biriga nisbatan joylashishi

UNDER, BEHIND & IN FRONT OF
• UNDER: The cat is under the table.
• BEHIND: Who is that man standing behind the tree?
• IN FRONT OF: There is a beautiful garden in front of our house.

NEXT TO, BESIDE, BETWEEN
Yonma-yon yoki o'rtada bo'lish holatlari:
• NEXT TO / BESIDE: Come and sit next to (beside) me.
• BETWEEN: Our house is between the bank and the shop.
• OPPOSITE: The cinema is opposite the park (yo'lning narigi tomonida).

Exercises (Homework)
102.1 Rasmlarga qarab predloglarni qo'ying.
1. The bin is under the desk.
2. The car is parked in front of the house.
3. I was sitting between my two friends.
4. The cinema is opposite the supermarket.
5. Who is that girl standing behind you?
6. Your shoes are under the bed.

102.2 Gaplarni mantiqan to'ldiring.
1. I can't see the screen. There's a tall man in front of me.
2. Don't stand behind the door. I can't open it.
3. The dictionary is on the shelf, above the desk.
4. In Britain, cars drive on the left.
5. Our flat is on the second floor. There's a shop below it.

💡 Pro Tip: Opposite vs In front of
Opposite - yuzma-yuz turish (masalan, stolning ikki tomonida yoki ko'chaning ikki betida).
In front of - shunchaki oldida turish.
