UNIT 112
Phrasal Verbs (2)
Keng tarqalgan frazal fe'llar

Quyida eng ko'p ishlatiladigan frazal fe'llar va ularning ma'nolari keltirilgan:

carry on (davom etmoq)
• Sorry for the interruption. Please carry on with your work.

find out (bilmoq / aniqlamoq)
• I need to find out what time the train leaves.

give up (tashlamoq / to'xtatmoq)
• He gave up smoking last year.

look after (qaramoq / parvarish qilmoq)
• Can you look after my dog while I'm away?

run out of (tugab qolmoq)
• We have run out of milk. Can you buy some?

turn down (rad etmoq / ovozni pasaytirmoq)
• He turned down the job offer.
• Please turn down the music.

Exercises (Homework)
112.1 Complete the sentences with the correct phrasal verb.
1. I need to find out the answer to this question.
2. We ran out of petrol, so we had to walk.
3. She looks after her elderly grandmother.
4. He decided to give up his bad habits.
5. They turned down my application.
6. Please carry on with your presentation.

112.2 Match the phrasal verb with its meaning.
1. look after → take care of
2. run out of → have no more of something
3. give up → stop doing something
4. find out → discover information

💡 Muhim eslatma:
"Run out of" har doim "of" predlogi bilan keladi va undan keyin narsa (milk, time, money) ishlatiladi.
