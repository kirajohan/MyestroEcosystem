UNIT 103
up, over, through
Harakat yo'nalishini bildiruvchi predloglar

UP and DOWN (Yuqoriga / Pastga)
Vertical yo'nalishdagi harakatni bildiradi:
• UP: We walked up the hill.
• DOWN: Be careful! Don't fall down the stairs.

OVER and UNDER (Ustidan / Tagidan)
Biror narsaning ustidan o'tish yoki tagidan o'tish:
• OVER: The plane flew over the mountains.
• UNDER: We drove under the bridge.

THROUGH and ROUND (Ichidan / Aylanib)
• THROUGH: Biror narsaning ichidan o'tish (tunnel, o'rmon).
  Example: The train went through the tunnel.
• ROUND: Biror narsaning atrofidan aylanib o'tish (bino, shahar).
  Example: We drove round the city centre.

Exercises (Homework)
103.1 Gaplarni harakat predloglari bilan to'ldiring.
1. The cat jumped over the wall.
2. We walked up the stairs to the first floor.
3. Go through the tunnel to reach the other side.
4. The car drove round the roundabout.
5. The moon goes round the earth.
6. Don't run down the hill; it's dangerous.

103.2 Choose the right preposition.
1. Suddenly a dog ran out of / through the house. → out of
2. They climbed up / over the wall into the garden. → over
3. I walked round / through the park to see the flowers. → through
4. The plane is flying over / down France right now. → over

💡 Pro Tip: Through vs Round
Through - biror narsaning ichidan o'tish (tunnel, oyna, o'rmon).
Round - biror narsaning atrofidan aylanib o'tish (bino, shahar, dunyo).
