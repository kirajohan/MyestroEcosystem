UNIT 107
Verb + Preposition (1)
Fe'l va undan keyin keladigan predloglar

Ingliz tilida ko'plab fe'llar doimiy ravishda ma'lum bir predlog bilan ishlatiladi. Bu birikmalarni butunlay yodlash kerak.

apologize for (uzr so'ramoq)
• He apologized for being late.

apply for (ariza topshirmoq)
• I applied for a new job.

belong to (tegishli bo'lmoq)
• Who does this book belong to?

depend on (bog'liq bo'lmoq)
• "Are you going?" "It depends on the weather."

listen to (quloq solmoq)
• Listen to me carefully.

look at (qaramoq)
• Look at this picture.

look for (qidirmoq)
• I'm looking for my keys.

pay for (to'lov qilmoq)
• I'll pay for the dinner.

wait for (kutmoq)
• I'm waiting for the bus.

Exercises (Homework)
107.1 Complete the sentences with the correct preposition.
1. I'm looking for my glasses. Have you seen them?
2. We waited for the train for an hour.
3. Who does this umbrella belong to?
4. I need to apologize for my mistake.
5. Can you pay for the tickets, please?
6. Listen to this song, it's great!

107.2 Choose the correct verb + preposition.
1. I (apologize for / apologize to) being late. → apologize for
2. She (listens to / listens at) music every day. → listens to
3. We are (waiting for / waiting to) you. → waiting for
