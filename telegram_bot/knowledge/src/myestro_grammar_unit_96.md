UNIT 96
at, on, in
Vaqt predloglari

AT (Aniq vaqtlar uchun)
Biz "at" predlogini soat vaqtlari va kunning aniq qismlari uchun ishlatamiz.
Examples:
• at 8 o'clock / at 10.30
• at midnight / at lunchtime
• at the weekend / at night / at Christmas

ON (Kun va sanalar uchun)
Biz "on" predlogini haftaning kunlari va aniq sanalar uchun ishlatamiz.
Examples:
• on Monday / on Saturdays
• on 25 April / on New Year's Day
• on Friday morning / on Sunday afternoon

IN (Uzoq vaqt oraliqlari uchun)
Biz "in" predlogini oylar, yillar, fasllar va kunning umumiy qismlari uchun ishlatamiz.
Examples:
• in April / in June
• in 2024 / in the 19th century
• in (the) summer / in the morning / in the evening

Exercises (Homework)
96.1 Put in AT, ON or IN.
1. Goodbye! See you on Friday.
2. I get up at 7 o'clock every morning.
3. I'm going away at the end of January.
4. My birthday is in October.
5. We often go to the beach in summer.
6. I'll phone you on Tuesday morning.
7. The stars shine at night.

96.2 Write AT, ON or IN for these times.
1. 8 o'clock → at
2. Monday → on
3. April → in
4. 15 July → on
5. 2025 → in
6. the evening → in
7. midnight → at
8. Saturday night → on

💡 Muhim eslatma:
"this / last / next / every" so'zlaridan oldin predlog ishlatilmaydi:
• I'll see you next Monday. (❌ on next Monday emas)
• They go away every summer.
