UNIT 105
Prepositions after Adjectives
Sifatdan keyin keladigan predloglar

Ba'zi sifatlar doimiy ravishda ma'lum bir predlog bilan ishlatiladi. Ularni yodlab olish muhim.

afraid of (qo'rqmoq)
• Are you afraid of spiders?
• Don't be afraid of asking questions.

angry with / at (jahli chiqmoq)
• She was angry with me for being late.
• He is angry at the situation.

bad / good at (yomon / yaxshi (biror ishda))
• I'm not very good at mathematics.
• She is good at learning languages.

excited about (hayajonlanmoq)
• Are you excited about your new job?

famous for (bilan mashhur)
• Italy is famous for its food.

interested in (qiziqmoq)
• Are you interested in history?

married to (uylangan / turmush qurgan)
• She is married to a doctor. (NOT: married with)

proud of (faxrlanmoq)
• We are very proud of our son.

similar to (o'xshash)
• Your phone is similar to mine.

worried about (xavotirda bo'lmoq)
• He is worried about his exams.

Exercises (Homework)
105.1 Complete the sentences with the correct preposition.
1. I'm not very good at remembering names.
2. Are you interested in art?
3. She is married to a famous actor.
4. Don't be afraid of the dark.
5. Italy is famous for its pizza and pasta.
6. I'm really excited about the trip.

105.2 Write sentences using the words in brackets.
1. (angry / me) → She is angry with me.
2. (proud / you) → I am proud of you.
3. (worried / the future) → They are worried about the future.

💡 Muhim eslatma:
"Married" so'zidan keyin har doim "to" ishlatiladi. "Married with" xato hisoblanadi.
