UNIT 98
before, after, during, while
Vaqt munosabatini ifodalovchi so'zlar

BEFORE and AFTER (... oldin / ... keyin)
Ushbu predloglardan keyin ot (noun) yoki -ing shaklidagi fe'l kelishi mumkin.
Examples:
• Everybody was nervous before the exam.
• I'll call you after work.
• Wash your hands before eating.

DURING (... davomida)
During predlogidan keyin har doim ot (noun) keladi. U biror narsa qachon sodir bo'lganini bildiradi.
Examples:
• I fell asleep during the film.
• We met a lot of people during our holiday.

WHILE (... davomida / -ayotgan vaqtda)
While bog'lovchisidan keyin har doim Ega + Fe'l (to'liq gap) keladi.
Examples:
• I fell asleep while I was watching the film.
• We met a lot of people while we were on holiday.

Exercises (Homework)
98.1 Gaplarni DURING yoki WHILE bilan to'ldiring.
1. I fell asleep during the film.
2. I fell asleep while I was watching the film.
3. We met a lot of people during our holiday.
4. We met a lot of people while we were on holiday.
5. The phone rang while we were having dinner.
6. I moved my head during the night.

98.2 Put in BEFORE or AFTER.
1. Take this medicine before you go to bed.
2. I felt tired after the long walk.
3. Brush your teeth before breakfast.
4. I'll see you after I get back from work.

💡 Muhim farq:
DURING + Ot (during the night)
WHILE + Gap (while I was sleeping)
