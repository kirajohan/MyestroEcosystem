UNIT 88
not as ... as
Solishtirishning muqobil usuli

Meaning and Structure
not as ... as konstruktsiyasi narsalarni solishtirganda "dek ... emas" ma'nosini beradi.
Formula: not as + Adjective + as

Examples:
• Rome is not as old as Athens. (= Athens is older)
• The car is not as expensive as the house.
• I'm not as tall as you. (= You are taller than me)

compare: than vs as ... as
Ma'no bir xil, lekin gap tuzilishi har xil bo'ladi:
Using THAN: Athens is older than Rome.
Using AS...AS: Rome is not as old as Athens.

common phrases
• the same as... (bilan bir xil): My hair is the same color as yours.
• as much as... (unchalik ko'p): I don't eat as much as you.

Exercises (Homework)
88.1 Rewrite these sentences using "not as ... as".
Athens is older than Rome. → Rome is not as old as Athens.
My car is faster than yours. → Your car is not as fast as mine.
I'm taller than her. → She is not as tall as me.
Tennis is more exciting than football. → Football is not as exciting as tennis.
The film was better than the book. → The book was not as good as the film.

88.2 Complete the sentences with "as ... as".
I'm sorry I'm late. I got here as fast as I could.
It's a very simple game. It's as easy as you think.
Can you send me the money as soon as possible?
I don't know as many people as you.

88.3 Complete with THE SAME AS.
My car is the same as yours.
David is the same age as me.
What would you like to drink? I'll have the same as you.
