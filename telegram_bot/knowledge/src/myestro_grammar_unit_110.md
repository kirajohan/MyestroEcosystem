UNIT 110
Verb + Preposition (4)
Fe'l va undan keyin keladigan predloglar (+ V-ing)

Ushbu predloglardan keyin fe'l kelganda, u har doim **-ing** shaklida (gerund) ishlatiladi.

look forward to (intizor bo'lmoq / kutmoq)
• I look forward to hearing from you. (NOT: to hear)
• We are looking forward to going on holiday.

be/get used to (ko'nikmoq / o'rganib qolmoq)
• I am used to waking up early.
• She can't get used to the cold weather.

prevent ... from (to'xtatmoq / xalaqit bermoq)
• The rain prevented us from playing tennis.

stop ... from (to'xtatmoq)
• Nothing can stop me from achieving my goals.

protect ... from (himoya qilmoq)
• This cream protects your skin from the sun.

apologize to ... for (uzr so'ramoq)
• He apologized to me for being late.

Exercises (Homework)
110.1 Complete the sentences with the correct preposition and V-ing.
1. I look forward to meeting you next week.
2. I am used to working long hours.
3. The noise prevented me from sleeping.
4. She apologized to her teacher for forgetting the homework.
5. This vaccine protects you from getting the disease.

110.2 Choose the correct form.
1. I'm not used to (drive / driving) on the left. → driving
2. He stopped me from (make / making) a mistake. → making
3. We are looking forward to (see / seeing) you. → seeing

💡 Muhim qoida:
"Look forward to" va "be used to" dagi "to" bu infinitive "to" emas, balki predlogdir. Shuning uchun undan keyin fe'lning -ing shakli keladi.
