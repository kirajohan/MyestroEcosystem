UNIT 92
always / usually / often
Mid-position (Fe'l bilan birga)

Always, usually, often, never, rarely, sometimes kabi ravishlar gapning o'rtasida, ya'ni asosiy fe'ldan oldin keladi.
Formula: Subject + Adverb + Verb

Examples:
• I always drink coffee in the morning.
• Helen usually goes to work by car.
• They often watch television in the evening.
• I never eat meat.
• Do you often go to the cinema?

With 'BE' (am / is / are)
Agar gapda BE fe'li (am/is/are) bo'lsa, ravish undan keyin keladi.
Formula: am / is / are + Adverb

Examples:
• I am always tired.
• They are usually at home on Sundays.
• It is often cold in winter.
• You are never on time!

With Auxiliary Verbs (Yordamchi fe'llar)
Agar gapda yordamchi fe'l (have, can, will) bo'lsa, ravish ular orasida keladi.
Examples:
• I have always lived in this city.
• You can never remember my name!
• We will usually be here at 9:00.

Exercises (Homework)
92.1 Put the words in the correct order.
(always / I / at 7:00 / get up) → I always get up at 7:00.
(often / she / television / watches) → She often watches television.
(usually / they / at home / are) → They are usually at home.
(never / we / to the cinema / go) → We never go to the cinema.
(always / has / lived / he / here) → He has always lived here.

92.2 Rewrite the sentences with the words in brackets.
My brother is late. (always) → My brother is always late.
I drink tea. (usually) → I usually drink tea.
We go to the beach. (often) → We often go to the beach.
You can park here. (never) → You can never park here.

92.3 Are the underlined words in the right order?
I always drink coffee. → RIGHT
Usually I go to work. → I usually go to work.
