UNIT 111
Phrasal Verbs (1)
Frazal fe'llar (Fe'l + Ravish)

Phrasal verb bu fe'l va undan keyin keladigan ravish (adverb) yoki predlogdan iborat birikma bo'lib, ularning ma'nosi asosiy fe'ldan farq qilishi mumkin.

Intransitive (To'ldiruvchisiz)
Bu fe'llar o'zidan keyin to'ldiruvchi talab qilmaydi.
Examples:
• The car broke down on the highway. (buzildi)
• Please sit down. (o'tiring)
• The plane took off at 9:00. (uchib ketdi)

Transitive (To'ldiruvchili)
Bu fe'llar o'zidan keyin to'ldiruvchi talab qiladi. Ular ajraluvchan (separable) yoki ajralmas (inseparable) bo'lishi mumkin.
Examples:
• I turned on the light. yoki I turned the light on. (yoqmoq)
• Please take off your shoes. yoki Please take your shoes off. (yechmoq)

Pronoun Rule (Olmosh qoidasi)
Agar to'ldiruvchi olmosh (it, them, me, him) bo'lsa, u fe'l va ravishning O'RTASIDA kelishi SHART.
Examples:
• I turned it on. (NOT: I turned on it)
• Take them off. (NOT: Take off them)

Exercises (Homework)
111.1 Put the words in the correct order.
1. (down / please / sit) → Please sit down.
2. (the light / turned / I / on) → I turned on the light. / I turned the light on.
3. (it / put / on) → Put it on.
4. (up / gave / he) → He gave up.

111.2 Rewrite the sentences using a pronoun (it/them).
1. I took off my coat. → I took it off.
2. Please turn off the TV. → Please turn it off.
3. She looked up the word. → She looked it up.

💡 Pro Tip:
Frazal fe'llarni lug'atdan o'qib yodlashdan ko'ra, ularni gap ichida ishlatib yodlagan ma'qul.
