UNIT 101
to, in, at
Places (3) - Yo'nalish va Joylashuv

TO (Direction - Yo'nalish)
Harakat va yo'nalishni (qayerga?) bildiradi.
Formula: go / come / return / walk to...

Examples:
• We're going to London.
• I want to go to Italy.
• We walked to the centre.
• What time do you go to bed?
• I went to a party.

IN / AT (Position - Joylashuv)
Harakatsiz holatni (qayerda?) bildiradi.
Formula: be / stay / do something in/at...

Examples:
• Piccadilly Circus is in London.
• My brother lives in Italy.
• I like reading in bed.
• The bus is at the airport.
• I met her at a party.

Special Cases (Maxsus holatlar)
• HOME: "Home" so'zi bilan "to" ishlatilmaydi: go home / come home. Lekin joylashuvda: at home.
• ARRIVE: Arrive in (davlat/shahar) / Arrive at (bino/joy).
• GET: Har doim "to" ishlatiladi: get to work / get to the hotel.

Exercises (Homework)
101.1 Put in TO, IN or AT.
1. We're going to London next week.
2. Piccadilly Circus is in London.
3. I want to go to Italy.
4. My brother lives in Italy.
5. The bus is going to the airport.
6. The bus is at the airport.
7. What time did you go to bed?

101.2 Complete the sentences with TO, IN or AT.
1. Ann stayed at her brother's house.
2. I'm tired. I'm going home. (No preposition!)
3. Sally didn't go to work yesterday.
4. I finished my work and then I went to a restaurant.
5. My brother lives in a small village.

💡 Eslatma:
Harakat bormi? (Go/Run/Walk) → TO ishlating.
Harakat yo'qmi? (Be/Stay/Live) → IN yoki AT ishlating.
