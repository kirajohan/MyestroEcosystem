UNIT 97
from ... to, until, since, for
Vaqt predloglari

FROM ... TO (... dan ... gacha)
Biz biror ishning qachon boshlanib, qachon tugaganini aytish uchun ishlatamiz.
Examples:
• We lived in Japan from 2010 to 2015.
• I work from Monday to Friday.

UNTIL (... gacha)
Ishning ma'lum bir vaqt nuqtasigacha davom etishini bildiradi.
Examples:
• Let's wait until it stops raining.
• I stayed in bed until half past nine.

SINCE (... dan beri)
Ishning boshlanish nuqtasini ko'rsatadi (asosan Present Perfect bilan).
Examples:
• We have lived in this house since 2018.
• It has been raining since I got up.

FOR (... davomida)
Vaqtning umumiy davomiyligini (qancha vaqt?) ko'rsatadi.
Examples:
• We have lived here for three days.
• She is going away for a week.

Exercises (Homework)
97.1 Gaplarni mos predloglar (from, to, until, since, for) bilan to'ldiring.
1. I lived in Japan from 2010 to 2015.
2. We have been waiting since 8 o'clock.
3. I stayed in bed until 10:00 this morning.
4. They have been married for ten years.
5. How long are you staying? Until Monday next week.
6. It has been raining since lunchtime.
7. I worked in the bank from 2018 to 2022.
8. I'll be away until Friday. I'll be back on Saturday.
9. We've lived in this city for a very long time.

97.2 SINCE yoki FOR ishlatib gaplarni davom ettiring.
1. (three days) We have been here for three days.
2. (last Monday) I haven't seen him since last Monday.
3. (ten years) They have lived in London for ten years.
4. (lunchtime) It has been raining since lunchtime.
5. (a long time) I've known her for a long time.
6. (2015) He has been a teacher since 2015.
