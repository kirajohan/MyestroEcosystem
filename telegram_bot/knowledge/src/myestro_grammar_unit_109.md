UNIT 109
Verb + Preposition (3)
Fe'l va undan keyin keladigan predloglar (Davomi)

Ingliz tilida ko'plab fe'llar ma'lum bir predlog bilan doimiy birikma hosil qiladi. Ularni butunlay yodlash kerak.

accuse ... of (ayblamoq)
• They accused him of stealing the money.

approve of (ma'qullamoq)
• I don't approve of his behavior.

consist of (iborat bo'lmoq)
• The UK consists of four countries.

depend on (bog'liq bo'lmoq)
• "Are you going?" "It depends on the weather."

remind ... of (eslatmoq / esga solmoq)
• You remind me of my brother.

specialize in (ixtisoslashmoq)
• This company specializes in software development.

thank ... for (uchun rahmat aytmoq)
• I thanked her for her help.

warn ... about (ogohlantirmoq)
• The doctor warned him about the risks.

Exercises (Homework)
109.1 Complete the sentences with the correct preposition.
1. This book consists of three parts.
2. I don't approve of smoking in public places.
3. She specializes in environmental law.
4. Thank you for inviting me to the party.
5. The police accused him of robbery.
6. Please remind me about the meeting tomorrow.

109.2 Write sentences using the words in brackets.
1. (depend / the weather) → It depends on the weather.
2. (remind / my father) → You remind me of my father.
3. (warn / the danger) → They warned us about the danger.

💡 Muhim eslatma:
"Consist" fe'lidan keyin har doim "of" keladi. "Consist from" yoki "consist in" xato hisoblanadi.
